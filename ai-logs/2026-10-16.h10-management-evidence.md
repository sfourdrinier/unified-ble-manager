# H10 management evidence probe

## Decision

The simulator no longer fabricates a successful empty read for the structurally readable but unmeasured Polar PFC feature characteristic (`6217ff4c`). The H10 capture scenario now performs a read-only probe of the canonical PFC service/feature UUIDs and records exact bytes as `values.polarPfcFeatures.raw`, or the structured read error.

PSFTP payloads and reset behavior remain unmodeled because the retained real-device fingerprints establish only GATT identity, properties, and descriptors. No writes, reset requests, or hypothetical payloads were added.
