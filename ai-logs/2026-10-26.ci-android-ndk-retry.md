# Android NDK setup retries (CI only)

- Symptom: the v5.1.3 publish run 40422058638 (attempt 1) failed only its
  "Source Expo Android (SDK 57)" gate, at "Setup Android SDK". The publisher was
  skipped; npm never received 5.1.3.
- Cause: the runner's `ndk;27.1.12297006` download stopped at 28%; sdkmanager
  unzipped the partial archive ("Error reading Zip content from a
  SeekableByteChannel") and exited 1. Neither sdkmanager nor
  `android-actions/setup-android` retries, and the same step stood in seven
  places across `ci.yml`, `publish.yml` and `publish-parallel-draft.yml`.
- Change: `.github/actions/setup-android-sdk` keeps `setup-android` for
  cmdline-tools, licences and platform-tools, then runs
  `scripts/ci/install-android-ndk.js`. It reads the NDK pin from
  `android/build.gradle`, retries up to four times with growing waits, removes
  the partial NDK and sdkmanager temp state between attempts, and succeeds only
  when `source.properties` reports the pinned revision. `ci.yml` and
  `.github/publish-serial-reference.yml` use it; both publishers are
  regenerated.
- Tests: `__tests__/InstallAndroidNdk.test.js` (truncated download, wrong
  revision after exit 0, bounded failure, already installed, unreadable
  properties); `ReleaseRetrySafety` pins every Android setup to the action.
- Not covered: `platform-tools` still installs once through `setup-android`
  (small and normally preinstalled on the runner image).
- Release: 5.1.3 recovered by an entire immutable-tag rerun of the same
  workflow; this change reaches publishing from the next tag onward.
